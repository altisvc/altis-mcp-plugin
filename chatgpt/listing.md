# ChatGPT listing — draft

**Name:** Altis

**Short description:** Altis research in ChatGPT.

**Long description:**
Search and read Altis research — company reports, market landscapes, scans, pulses, signals and
expert-call transcripts — exactly as it is published on altis.vc. Ask about a company, a sector or
a theme; answers cite the Altis pieces they come from, with links. Read-only. Sign in with your Altis
account when you connect: any account can search what Altis covers and has published, and an all-access
account reads the research itself.

**Example prompts:**
- Brief me on Mercor in 300 words using Altis research.
- What has Altis published on nuclear fission since September?
- Open the Altis report on Harvey and summarise the competitive section.
- Which companies in AI training data does Altis cover, and which have a full report?
