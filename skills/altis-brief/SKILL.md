# A short Altis brief on one company

Using the altis-research skill to find and read the research, write a brief of about 300 words on
the company the user named:

- its coverage rung (and say so plainly if Altis has not published research on it yet),
- the thesis in one paragraph,
- the two most important debates and Altis's view on each,
- links to every piece you used.

If the user asks for a different length, use theirs. For a brief ahead of a meeting, use the
altis-meeting-prep skill instead.
